# SolarMitra — Government & Institutional Outreach Guide

How to pitch SolarMitra to the people who actually own the problem it solves
(rooftop solar adoption, subsidy awareness, financing, installer trust).

> ⚠️ Honesty rules before you send anything
> - SolarMitra is a **hackathon prototype with simulated data** (demo dataset, simulated OCR,
>   simulated GSTIN/PAN/penny-drop checks). Say so in the first mail. Officials respond well to
>   "working prototype, happy to demo"; they stop reading at "we have launched a platform".
> - Never claim affiliation with MNRE, PM Surya Ghar, a DISCOM, or Satin Finserv beyond the
>   hackathon you actually took part in (SANKALP by Satin Finserv — The Climate Edition).
> - Don't attach anything containing real personal data. Attach a 1-page PDF + a demo link.

## 2. The mail itself — rules that get replies

1. **Subject line that states the ask, not the product.**
2. **Under 200 words.** One screen on a phone. Officials read on phones.
3. **Lead with their problem, not your tech stack.** Nobody cares that it's React + Express.
4. **One ask, one link, one attachment.** Ask for a 15-minute demo, nothing else.
5. **Be explicit that it's a prototype with simulated data** and that you're not selling anything.
6. **Sign with your real name, college, phone, city.** Anonymity reads as spam.
7. **Follow up once after 10–14 working days**, then move to the next door. Don't mass-Cc 20 officials
   on one mail — send individually.

## 4. Before you hit send — a 20-minute checklist

- [ ] **A live demo link.** Deploy the client + API (Render/Railway/Fly + Vercel/Netlify) with
      `DEMO_MODE=true`. A localhost screenshot is not a demo.
- [ ] **Remove demo credentials from anything public-facing**, or label them clearly as demo-only.
- [ ] **A 90-second screen recording** (unlisted YouTube). Many officials will watch this and never
      open the link.
- [ ] **A 1-page PDF**: problem · what it does · screenshot · what's simulated · who you are.
- [ ] **A "what's real vs simulated" line** in the README and the one-pager. This builds more
      credibility than any feature list.
- [ ] Mail from a **sober address** (`firstname.lastname@…`), ideally your college ID.
- [ ] Attach **nothing over ~5 MB**; gov mail servers reject large attachments.
